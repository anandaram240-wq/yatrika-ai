## 📋 Recording Checklist Before You Hit Record
1. **Resolution & Audio:** 1080p 60fps screen recording (OBS / Loom / QuickTime). Clear microphone audio (no echo).
2. **Browser State:** Chrome open at `http://localhost:5173` (or deployed Vercel URL), zoom level set to 100%, bookmarks bar hidden (`Cmd+Shift+B` on Mac / `Ctrl+Shift+B` on Windows).
3. **Sound Check:** Ensure system audio is recorded so that when you click the 🔊 audio phrase buttons, the native Kannada speech synthesis is clearly audible in the video.
4. **Slide Deck Ready:** Have `YatriKa_AI_Karnataka_Travel_Planner_Presentation.pptx` or PDF open in fullscreen on another tab or split-screen for the 30-second problem intro.

## 💡 Top Tips for Delivering a 1st-Place Demo Video
1. **Speak with Energy:** Enthusiastic, clear delivery signals confidence and technical mastery.
2. **Audio Volume:** Ensure the browser tab audio (the Kannada TTS voice) is clearly picked up by your recording software.
3. **No Dead Air:** Keep the mouse moving purposefully to each feature as you speak about it.
4. **Show the Tests:** Judges love seeing terminal proof (`38 passed`) because it proves you didn't just write a Figma mockup — you built production software.
