## 2. The Three Defensible Moats

### Moat 1: Deterministic Zero-Latency Kannada Linguistic Engine
Commercial AI apps make high-latency (1.5s – 3.0s), costly API calls to OpenAI/Gemini for every user keystroke. YatriKa AI runs a client-side deterministic parsing pipeline in TypeScript (`<50ms` execution) that cleans Kannada Unicode, extracts grammatical case markers (`-ನಿಂದ`, `-ಗೆ`), classifies regional dialects, and matches itineraries with 0 API cost.

### Moat 2: Real Western Ghats Offline Survival Architecture
Tourism in Karnataka's most scenic destinations (Kudremukh, Mullayanagiri, Agumbe, Jog Falls, Dandeli) frequently suffers complete cellular blackout. YatriKa AI packages the entire trip—turn-by-turn road geometry, essential Kannada audio phrases, and emergency contacts (112, 108, 1077, 1926)—into a verified, checksum-protected IndexedDB package that functions entirely in Airplane Mode.

### Moat 3: Official Data Provenance & Policy Alignment
Unlike platforms that scrape unverified blogs, every fee and rule in YatriKa AI is cited against official government orders:
- **ASI cashless pricing:** ₹40 vs ₹50 physical cash at Hampi.
- **KFD Aranya Vihaara eco-cess:** ₹50 checkpost permit at Kaimara for Mullayanagiri.
- **Shakti Scheme:** Distinction between zero-fare KSRTC Ordinary/Sarige services and paid luxury Airawat Club Class / Flybus services.
