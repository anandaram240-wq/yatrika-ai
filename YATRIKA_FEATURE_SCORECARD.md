## 2. Selection Rationale

1. **Why System A (Kannada Multimodal Agent) was chosen:**
   - Directly nails the hackathon theme: *"Code. Create. Celebrate Kannada"*.
   - Transforms the application from a passive map into an interactive conversational companion that understands colloquial Kannada, regional dialects, and budget constraints.
   - Provides an immediate "Aha!" moment in the first 45 seconds of the live demonstration.

2. **Why System B (Offline Survival Mode) was chosen:**
   - Solves the single most severe practical barrier in Karnataka tourism: Western Ghats network blackouts.
   - Demonstrates genuine engineering depth (IndexedDB, checksum validation, in-memory recovery, storage quotas).
   - High impact for traveler safety via the offline 112/108/1077/1926 emergency directory.

3. **Rejection of Unfinished Superfluous Features:**
   - Avoided building superficial blockchain passports or mock booking payment gateways that distract from core software reliability and linguistic excellence.
